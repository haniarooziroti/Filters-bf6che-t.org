The cheat market has a reputation, and some of it is earned. Slick pages, glowing reviews, then silence the moment you need help. You can protect yourself with a few simple checks.

## Red flags

**"Undetected forever."** Nobody can promise that. If a page does, it is selling a feeling, not a product.

**No live status.** If you cannot see whether the product is working right now, you are buying blind.

**No way to reach a human.** A contact form that goes nowhere is the oldest trick there is.

**Vague feature lists.** "Advanced aimbot, powerful ESP" tells you nothing. A real seller lists the options: FOV, smoothness, bone selection, skeleton, snaplines, config system.

**Pressure.** Countdown timers and "last key" banners are noise.

## Green flags

- A published feature list you can compare with other sellers
- Live status you can check before paying
- Free updates during your key
- Instant delivery and a portal for downloads
- An HWID reset option
- Support by ticket and Discord, available around the clock
- Several normal payment methods, including card and PayPal

## A quick test

Before buying, ask one question through the support channel. How fast and how clearly do they answer? You just learned how they will treat you after payment.

## Do not buy on price alone

A cheap key with no updates is expensive the first time the game patches.
