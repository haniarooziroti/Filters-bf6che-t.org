Free sounds great until you ask the obvious question: if nobody is paying for it, who is keeping it working?

That is the real difference between free and paid. It is not about the price tag. It is about who is responsible when the game updates at 2 a.m. and your cheat stops loading.

## What free downloads usually lack

**Updates.** Games patch. A cheat that is not maintained stops working, and there is nobody to fix it.

**Support.** Something breaks and you are alone. No ticket system, no Discord, no human to answer.

**Accountability.** With a paid seller there is a business that wants you back. With an anonymous file there is nothing.

**A clear feature list.** Free downloads often hide what they do. A paid product tells you: FOV, smoothness, bone selection, skeleton, snaplines, config system.

**A safe way to move PCs.** Paid keys offer an HWID reset. Free files rarely do.

## What you get when you pay

- Instant delivery after payment
- Free updates during your key
- Support by ticket and Discord
- An HWID reset in the portal
- 29 modules across aimbot, visuals and misc options
- Windows 10/11 support on Intel or AMD

## The price is lower than you think

The cheapest way in is a one-week key at $15, which lets you test the whole thing. A month is $35 and lifetime is $150.
