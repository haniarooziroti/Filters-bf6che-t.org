Let us skip the sales pitch for a minute. Anyone who tells you a Battlefield 6 cheat can never be banned is not being straight with you. The honest answer is simpler, and more useful.

## Risk exists. Here is why.

Cheats and anti-cheat systems are in a constant race. A game patch or anti-cheat update can change what is safe overnight. That is why serious sellers publish a live status and tell you to check it, instead of promising permanent protection. If a page says "undetected forever," treat it as marketing, not information.

## What actually moves the needle

You cannot control the anti-cheat. You can control your own behavior. These are the habits players talk about most.

**Check status before every session.** Not once a week. Every time. A cheat that was fine yesterday might not be today.

**Keep your settings believable.** A tiny FOV and moderate smoothness look very different from a huge circle and an instant snap. Our [config preset guide](/blog/battlefield-6-config-presets/) shows the difference.

**Do not turn on everything.** More features means more things that can look odd. Use what you need.

**Watch your play, not just your settings.** Perfect headshots through smoke on every kill gets noticed by other players, who report you. Reports matter.

**Keep your license tidy.** If you change PCs, use the HWID reset in your portal rather than improvising. See the [HWID reset guide](/blog/battlefield-6-hwid-reset-guide/).

## What you should not trust

- Pages that promise 100% safety
- Sellers with no live status
- Anyone telling you to ignore updates
- Setups with no support if something goes wrong

Our [provider checklist](/blog/how-to-choose-a-battlefield-6-cheat-provider/) goes deeper on spotting these.

## So, is it worth it?

That is your call. Using cheats breaks the game's rules and can cost you your account. If you decide to go ahead, choose a seller that is honest about status, offers free updates and has real support. You can see current status and plans on the [Battlefield 6 product page](/go/battlefield-6/), and other players share their experiences in the [forum](/forum/).
