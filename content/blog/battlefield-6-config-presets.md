Two players. Same cheat. One looks like a sharp player having a great night. The other looks like a bot. The difference is nearly always the config.

Here are two starting points. Copy one, save it, and tweak from there. The numbers are described in plain terms because the right value depends on your own setup.

## Preset A: Cautious

**Goal:** blend in, keep control.

- **Aim bind:** hold a mouse button, not Aim Always
- **FOV:** small, so it only reacts near your crosshair
- **Smoothness:** high, so the pull is slow and steady
- **Distance:** moderate, nothing at extreme range
- **Bone:** body or neck, with head only when it feels natural
- **Visible check:** on
- **ESP:** box and distance, nothing else
- **Max distance:** moderate

This one is built for longer sessions and for anyone nervous about attention.

## Preset B: Aggressive

**Goal:** maximum information and speed.

- **Aim bind:** hold or Aim Always depending on taste
- **FOV:** larger
- **Smoothness:** lower
- **Distance:** long
- **Bone:** head
- **Visible check:** on
- **ESP:** box, name, distance, health bar, skeleton and snaplines
- **Max distance:** long

It is stronger, and also far easier for other players to notice. Use it in casual matches or bot-heavy modes, not where eyes are on you.

## How to build your own

1. Load Preset A and play one match.
2. Change only one setting, such as smoothness.
3. Play again. Did it feel better?
4. Save the result as a new config.
5. Repeat.

Saving each step means you can always go back. The config system supports save, load, share and delete, so keep a few versions around.
