It worked yesterday. Today nothing. Take a breath. Most problems are fixable in a few minutes, and the cause is usually one of the same handful of things.

## 1. Check live status first

If the game updated, the cheat may be waiting on an update too. Look at the status on the product page before touching anything else. If it shows an issue, wait. Updates during your key are free.

## 2. Confirm your system

You need Windows 10 or 11 with an Intel or AMD processor. If you are on something else, that is your answer.

## 3. Follow the portal guide again

Your portal has the loader and the setup guide. Re-read the steps and compare them with what you did. Small skipped steps cause most failed installs.

## 4. Did your hardware change?

New parts, a new PC or a Windows reinstall can break license recognition. Use the HWID reset in your portal. Our [HWID guide](/blog/battlefield-6-hwid-reset-guide/) covers it step by step.

## 5. Menu will not open?

Check your menu bind. If you changed it and forgot, that is the problem. If you cannot remember, reload your saved config.

## 6. Things look wrong in game

Too many ESP elements can make the screen unreadable. Lower the max distance and turn off extras. Our [ESP guide](/blog/battlefield-6-esp-wallhack-explained/) shows a clean setup.

## 7. Still stuck? Ask properly

Open a support ticket or join Discord. Include:

- What you were doing when it failed
- Any error text you saw
- Whether your hardware or Windows changed recently
- What your status page says

Clear details get faster answers, and support is available around the clock.

## Prevent most problems

Check status before each session, keep a saved preset and use the [setup guide](/blog/battlefield-6-cheat-setup-guide/) when you move PCs. Need a fresh start? Visit the [Battlefield 6 product page](/go/battlefield-6/), or compare notes in the [forum](/forum/).
