Not everyone wants to chase a ranked ladder. Some people just want to log in after work, drop into a big map and feel powerful for an hour. Battlefield 6 has plenty of room for that, and the right options make it a lot more fun.

## Bots versus players

The cheat treats them separately. **Bot ESP** marks AI-controlled targets, and **Target Bots** lets the aimbot select them. **Player ESP** handles human opponents. You choose which to switch on, so you can mix and match.

## Why casual players like it

**Learn the map.** With ESP running, you see where enemies spawn and travel. That teaches you routes and choke points much faster than dying repeatedly.

**Practice recoil and positioning.** Knowing where targets are lets you work on your own movement and aim without constant surprise.

**Relax.** Some nights you want the fun of winning, not the stress of grinding.

## A good casual setup

- Bot ESP and Player ESP on
- Box, distance and health bar for readability
- Target Bots on, Target Teams set so you do not select friendly players
- Visible check on
- Larger FOV is fine here, since the aggressive approach suits casual lobbies

Our [aggressive preset](/blog/battlefield-6-config-presets/) is a good starting point. Save it as its own config and swap back to a cautious one when you play with other humans.

## A word of honesty

Bot-heavy modes are not risk-free either. Status can change after updates and rules still apply. Read the [ban risk article](/blog/battlefield-6-ban-risk-explained/) so you know what you are signing up for, and check live status before each session.
