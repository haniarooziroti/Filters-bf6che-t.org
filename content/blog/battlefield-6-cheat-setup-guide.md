Buying a cheat should take less time than loading into a match. It usually does, as long as you do things in the right order. Here is the route from checkout to your first game.

## Step 1: check status first

Before you pay, confirm the product shows as online. Status is current only, and it can change after a game or anti-cheat update. This takes ten seconds and saves you grief.

## Step 2: pick a plan

One week, one month or lifetime. Not sure? Our [plan comparison](/blog/battlefield-6-cheat-pricing/) shows who each one suits. If you want to test first, start with the week.

## Step 3: pay

Card, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Cash App, Amazon Pay and crypto are all available. Delivery is instant, so your key unlocks as soon as payment clears.

## Step 4: install from the portal

Your portal holds the loader and the setup guide. Follow the guide as written. Use a Windows 10 or 11 PC with an Intel or AMD processor. If a step is unclear, do not guess. Open a ticket or ask on Discord.

## Step 5: load a cautious preset

When the menu opens, resist the urge to switch everything on. Start with Player ESP, distance and a small aimbot FOV with moderate smoothness. Turn on the visible check. Save that as your first config. If you need ideas, our [preset guide](/blog/battlefield-6-config-presets/) gives you two to copy.

## Step 6: play and adjust

Jump into a match and change one setting at a time. Learn your menu bind and your unload bind before you need them. Our [settings explainer](/blog/battlefield-6-aimbot-esp-features/) tells you what each slider does.

## A few habits that help

- Check live status before every session, especially after updates
- Keep your config saved so you can roll back
- Know how to reset your HWID if you change PCs, covered in the [HWID reset guide](/blog/battlefield-6-hwid-reset-guide/)

## Ready?

Grab your key from the [Battlefield 6 checkout page](/go/battlefield-6/). Stuck on anything? The [forum](/forum/) is full of players who have done it before.
